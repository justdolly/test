Our Policy

ANIMEX takes copyright infringement seriously. We comply with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) and respond promptly to takedown requests from rights holders and their authorized representatives.

All anime metadata displayed on ANIMEX — including titles, descriptions, scores, and artwork thumbnails — is sourced from the AniList public API and is used for informational purposes only.

Filing a DMCA Takedown Request

To file a valid DMCA notice, your request must include:

  • Your full legal name and contact information (email, address, phone)
  • Identification of the copyrighted work you believe is being infringed
  • The specific URL(s) on ANIMEX where the allegedly infringing content appears
  • A statement that you have a good faith belief that the use is unauthorized
  • A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the rights holder or authorized to act on their behalf
  • Your physical or electronic signature

Incomplete requests may not be processed. We reserve the right to request additional verification.

Counter-Notice

If you believe content was removed in error, you may file a counter-notice. Counter-notices must include your name, address, phone number, the specific content that was removed, a statement under penalty of perjury that the content was removed by mistake or misidentification, and your consent to jurisdiction.

Submit a Takedown Request

We aim to respond to valid DMCA requests within 48–72 business hours.
