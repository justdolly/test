Information We Collect

ANIMEX is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal information necessary to provide the service.

Information you provide:

  • Display name and profile information (if you create an account)
  • Watch history and list data (stored locally or on our servers if you sign in)
  • Anime requests and contact form submissions

Information collected automatically:

  • Basic usage data (pages visited, features used) — anonymized
  • Browser type and device type for compatibility purposes
  • General location data (country level only, for content availability)

We do not collect your IP address for tracking purposes, and we do not build individual behavioral profiles.

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information solely to operate and improve ANIMEX:

  • Providing and personalizing the streaming experience
  • Remembering your preferences (language, quality settings)
  • Maintaining your watchlist and watch history
  • Responding to support and contact requests
  • Improving site performance and fixing bugs

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell your data to any third party under any circumstances.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share anonymized, aggregated data (such as total active users or most-watched genres) for site analytics — this data cannot be used to identify you.

We may disclose information if required by law, such as in response to a valid court order or government request.

Cookies & Local Storage

ANIMEX uses browser local storage to save your preferences and watch progress locally on your device. This data never leaves your browser unless you are signed in to an account.

We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising cookies. Any cookies used are strictly functional — for example, keeping you signed in between sessions.

Third-Party Services

ANIMEX uses the following third-party services:

  • AniList API — provides anime metadata, scores, and descriptions. Subject to AniList's Terms of Service.
  • Google Fonts — loads the Clash Display and Satoshi typefaces. Subject to Google's font service privacy policy.
  • Font Awesome CDN — loads icon assets. No tracking.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these third-party services.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Export your watch history and list data
  • Opt out of any optional data collection

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact page or email privacy@animex.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through our contact page or email privacy@animex.com.

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be noted at the top of this page with an updated date.
