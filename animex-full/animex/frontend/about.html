What is ANIMEX?

ANIMEX is a self-hosted anime streaming frontend designed to give you a premium viewing experience. Built from scratch with a focus on design, performance, and usability — no bloat, no ads, no nonsense.

The platform pulls anime metadata from the AniList API, giving you accurate scores, descriptions, character info, and related titles for every show in the library.
